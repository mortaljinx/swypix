Swypix Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: Swypix works entirely on your phone. It does not collect your photos or any personal information, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and does not send your photos anywhere.

Who we are

Swypix is an Android app for reviewing and organising photos and videos by swiping. It is published by mortaljinx. You can contact the developer through the contact details on the Swypix page on Google Play.

What Swypix can see

With your permission, Swypix reads the photos and videos on your phone so it can show them to you. You can allow all photos and videos or only ones you pick, and you can change this at any time in Android Settings. The photos are read on your phone and are never uploaded.

What Swypix stores

To remember your choices, Swypix keeps a small database in its private storage on your phone. It holds:

  • which photos you have reviewed, favourited or moved to the Swypix trash, and when;
  • names, sizes, dates and similar details of those photos (not the photos themselves);
  • your settings, such as theme, accent colour and left-handed mode;
  • a cache used to find duplicate photos.

This information stays on your phone. Uninstalling Swypix or clearing its data removes it.

Locked folder

If you move a photo or video to the locked folder, Swypix copies it into the app's private storage and encrypts it with a key held in your phone's secure hardware (the Android Keystore). The folder opens only after you confirm your screen lock. Because the files live inside the app, uninstalling Swypix or clearing its data permanently deletes everything in the locked folder. We cannot recover it.

Backups you create

The backup feature saves your favourites and reviewed list to a file in a location you choose. Swypix does not upload it anywhere. What you do with that file is up to you.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Swypix hands the photo or video to the app you choose, such as Messages or email. That app's privacy policy then applies. Swypix does not share anything on its own.

Deleting photos

Photos you swipe away wait in the Swypix trash for up to 30 days. Permanently deleting them always requires your approval through a prompt from Android itself.

Purchases

Swypix Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment and shares only purchase confirmation with the app. Swypix never sees your payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics services.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not upload your photos or videos.

Children

Swypix is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
